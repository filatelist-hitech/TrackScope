# QA Audio Dataset

Required deterministic fixtures:

- 170, 180, 190, 200, and 220 BPM clean synthetic tracks
- 100 BPM half-time trap
- 400 BPM double-time trap
- silence
- white noise
- pink noise
- clipped microphone
- breakdown without kick
- dense hitech bassline simulation
- unstable club recording simulation

Reports must include expected BPM, detected BPM, error, confidence, lock state, signal quality, candidates, pass/fail, and notes.
